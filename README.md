## 🎯 What is this?

> No more *"so… what do you wanna play?"* for 45 minutes while the pizza gets cold.

**DesliderClaude** is a tiny web app your friend group uses to pick a boardgame together — **asynchronously**. Over the course of an afternoon, everyone opens a link on their phone, swipes 👍 / 👎 on the candidate games, and a live ranking updates as votes come in. When the host is ready, the top of the list is tonight's pick. 🎉

Not a review site. Not a rating service. Just: *"of these 12 games, which do we actually want to play tonight?"*

---

## 🚀 How it works

**Playing tonight?** Start a game night, choose games from your collection or add them by name, and share the voting link. Friends join with a display name and vote yes or no. They can change their votes until the host closes voting. Returning guests can select their existing name; guest identities are shared, so anyone with the invitation can resume them. Link your guest to an account to require sign-in while keeping your votes. Accounts can participate without hosting.

**Planning a weekend?** Create a gathering and share one invitation. Everyone adds games directly to the shared bring list, then visits a separate swipe page to choose what they would play. New games join the same ongoing vote, and voting progress shows who has finished the current list. The host closes voting when the group is ready, locking votes and game additions.

No date is required. Browse saved BoardGameGeek collections and GeekLists, or search BGG by name. Tap a game to add it to your night or gathering; manual entry remains available.

## 🛠️ Built with

- **.NET 10** and **Blazor**, with server-rendered pages and touch-friendly voting
- **SQLite** for game nights, gatherings, and voting history
- **BoardGameGeek** imports for saved game libraries
