Self-hosted font sources
Retrieved 2026-10-29 from Google Fonts. WOFF2 files are unmodified.
Latin and Latin Extended subsets cover German umlauts and both forms of sharp S.
Outfit and JetBrains Mono use variable fonts; Barlow Condensed uses static weights.
All fonts use SIL Open Font License 1.1; license texts are bundled alongside the fonts.
